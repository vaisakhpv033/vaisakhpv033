### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a passionate **Full-Stack Developer & AI Systems Engineer** based in Kochi, India. Transitioning with an engineering background into software development, I specialize in architecting high-performance web systems, real-time voice agents, and intelligent LLM workflows.

- 🔭 **Current Focus**: Real-time conversational pipelines, LiveKit audio agents, speech fine-tuning (Conformer/ASR), and low-latency microservices.
- 💻 **Core Tech**: Python, TypeScript, FastAPI, Django, React, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Docker.
- 🧠 **AI & Real-Time**: Voice AI pipelines, WebRTC, LLM agents, RAG architectures, and multimodal analyzers.
